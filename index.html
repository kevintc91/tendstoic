❦Tend

Ancient wisdom, modern practice.

"You have power over your mind, not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength."

Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 12.22

Tend is a small Android app for daily Stoic practice. Each day, one quote from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, or Epictetus, translated into plain modern English, with a real-world example and a question to sit with.

No streaks. No badges. No audience. A quiet place to read a hard-won idea and write a short reflection of your own. Your journal stays on your phone, nothing leaves your device, and no account is required.

What you get

  • One quote per day, delivered at the time you choose
  • A modern translation, a contemporary example, and a prompt to reflect on
  • A home screen widget so today's quote sits where you can see it
  • Save quotes that resonate, find them again in the Archive
  • A year from now, the reflection you wrote today surfaces back on the same calendar date
  • Pause notifications for a day, a week, or longer, when you need quiet
  • A full year of curated entries, no day-to-day recycling
  • Fully private. No accounts, no cloud syncing of your writing, no audience
